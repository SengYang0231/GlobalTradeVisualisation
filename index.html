①  The global trading landscape

Trade is the engine of modern economies. But not all countries trade equally — wealth, geography, and policy all shape how much a nation exports. This section sets the global stage before zooming in on Malaysia.

Who trades the most? A world of exports

Countries shaded by merchandise exports as a share of GDP (%, latest available year). Darker shades indicate higher trade dependency. Malaysia is highlighted in amber.

Top trading economies — where does Malaysia rank?

Malaysia ranks among the world's most trade-open economies. Its trade-to-GDP ratio far exceeds most comparable nations.

Richer countries trade more — but Malaysia punches above its weight

Malaysia's trade openness significantly exceeds what its income level alone would predict, making it a trade-dependent outlier.

②  Malaysia's trade journey over time

From a resource-exporting economy to a high-tech manufacturing hub, Malaysia's trade has grown dramatically since 1990. Global crises left visible scars, but recovery was always swift.

Three decades of growth — exports, imports, and the crises in between

Exports have consistently outpaced imports. The shaded gap shows Malaysia's trade surplus over time.

Malaysia has run a trade surplus every year since 1998

The 1997 Asian Financial Crisis caused Malaysia's only sustained deficit in modern trade history. Every year since 1998 has been a surplus.

③  Who does Malaysia trade with?

China, Singapore, and the United States dominate Malaysia's trade network. But those rankings have shifted dramatically over two decades, reflecting broader geopolitical and economic realignments in Asia.

Malaysia's trade web — flows to its top partners

Arc thickness represents trade volume (USD billions, 2023). Red arcs indicate export-heavy relationships (Malaysia exports more); dark arcs indicate import-heavy ones (Malaysia imports more).

China rises, Japan falls — partner rankings shift over two decades

China overtook the United States as Malaysia's top trading partner around 2010 and has stayed there since.

Surplus with the West, deficit with the East

Malaysia runs surpluses with most Western economies and ASEAN neighbours, but deficits with China and energy exporters in the Middle East.

④  What does Malaysia export?

Electronics dominate Malaysia's export basket. But the mix has shifted significantly since 2000 — with petroleum rising sharply and electronics' share declining, reflecting Malaysia's evolving industrial structure.

Electronics dominate — Malaysia's export basket in 2024

Electrical and electronic products account for nearly half of all Malaysian merchandise exports.

A shifting basket — how Malaysia's exports changed from 2000 to 2023

Machinery's share fell from 63% to 46%, while petroleum nearly doubled — a structural shift over two decades.

Two decades of exports — the rise and fall of each sector

Machinery and transport equipment has dominated throughout. Petroleum surged during the 2000s commodity boom before receding. Animal and vegetable oils (palm oil) remain steady.
